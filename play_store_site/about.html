About Alkitab

Alkitab is a dedicated Quran application designed to provide a seamless and immersive reading, listening, and studying experience. Our goal is to make the Holy Quran accessible and aesthetically pleasing for users worldwide.

Data Sources & Attribution

To ensure accuracy and reliability, Alkitab utilizes data from widely recognized and respected third-party sources. We do not generate the Quranic text, translations, or audio recitations ourselves.

Our application integrates legitimate and public APIs from reputable Islamic institutions and trusted open-source projects.

We do not have control over the original data and cannot guarantee its absolute perfection, although these sources are generally considered authentic and reliable.
