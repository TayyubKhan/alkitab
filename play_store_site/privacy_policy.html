Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Alkitab ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information in connection with your use of our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from you. The App is designed to be used without creating an account or providing personal details.

Local Data Storage

To provide features such as bookmarks, reading progress, and settings, we store data locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers and remains solely on your device unless you choose to back it up manually.

2. Permissions

The App may require certain permissions to function correctly:

  • Storage/Photos/Media: Required only if you choose to save or share images of verses.
  • Internet Access: Required to download audio recitations or translation updates.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Examples include Google Play Services for analytics or crash reporting to help us improve the App stability.

4. Security

We value your trust in using our App. Since we do not collect personal information, your privacy is naturally protected. For locally stored data, we rely on the security features of your device's operating system.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
